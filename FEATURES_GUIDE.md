# Study Groups & Content Sharing Features Guide

## Overview
This guide shows you exactly where to find and use the new study group management and content sharing features in the AI Study Companion.

---

## 🎯 Feature 1: Study Groups Management

### 1.1 Create a Study Group
1. Go to **"Study Groups"** in the left sidebar
2. Click the **"New Group"** button (top-right, purple button)
3. Fill in:
   - **Group Name** (e.g., "Biology 101 Study Crew")
   - **Description** (optional - e.g., "Study group for Biology chapter 3-5")
4. Click **"Create"**
5. ✅ Your group is created! You are automatically the owner.

### 1.2 Invite Members to Your Group
1. Go to **"Study Groups"** in the sidebar
2. Click on your group name to open it
3. On the **Members** section, click **"Invite Member"** button (purple, top-right)
4. Enter member email addresses:
   - Type an email and press **Enter** to add it
   - Add multiple emails one by one
   - See the list of added emails below the input
5. Click **"Invite"** to send invitations
6. ✅ Members will see this group in their "Study Groups" when they accept

### 1.3 Remove Members from Your Group
1. Go to your group (Study Groups → Click group name)
2. In the **Members** section, find the member you want to remove
3. Click the **X button** on the right side of their name
4. Confirm the deletion
5. ✅ Member is removed and can no longer see the group

### 1.4 View Group Members
1. Go to your group
2. The **Members** section shows:
   - Member name and email
   - Role (Owner or Member)
   - Remove button (for members only, owner can't remove themselves)

## 📚 Feature 2: Add Materials to Groups

### 2.1 Add Flashcard Sets to Your Group
1. Go to your group (Study Groups → Click group name)
2. In the **Shared Materials** section, click **"Add Materials"** button (blue, top-right)
3. Select **"Flashcards"** tab
4. Fill in:
   - **Set Name** (e.g., "Chapter 3 Vocabulary")
   - **Description** (optional)
5. Add flashcard IDs:
   - You can find flashcard IDs by going to your lectures and viewing flashcards
   - Paste each ID and press Enter
   - See all added IDs listed below
6. Click **"Add Materials"** to add them to the group
7. ✅ All group members can now see these flashcards!

### 2.2 Add Quiz Sets to Your Group
Same process as flashcards, but:
1. Select the **"Quizzes"** tab in the modal
2. Add quiz IDs (you took these in your quizzes)
3. All members can now practice these quizzes

### 2.3 Remove Materials from Group
1. Go to your group
2. In **Shared Materials** section, find the material
3. Click the **trash icon** on the right
4. Confirm deletion
5. ✅ Material is removed from the group (doesn't delete it from your library!)

### 2.4 View Group Materials
1. Go to your group
2. **Shared Materials** section shows:
   - **Flashcard Sets**: Name and description
   - **Quiz Sets**: Name and description
   - Both are organized in separate tabs

## 🔗 Feature 3: Share Flashcards & Quizzes

### 3.1 Share from Lecture View
1. Go to **"Lectures"** in the sidebar
2. Click on any lecture to view it
3. At the top-right of the header, click the **"Share"** button (purple)
4. This opens the Share Modal with options:
   - **Public Link**: Make accessible to anyone without login
   - **Share with Users**: Share with specific users in your app

### 3.2 Share from Flashcard Review
1. Go to **"Lectures"** → Click a lecture → Click **"Review"** to study flashcards
2. While reviewing, click the **"Share"** button (top-right, purple)
3. Choose:
   - **Public Link**: Generate a shareable link (copy to clipboard)
   - **Targeted Users**: Search and select specific users to share with

### 3.3 Share from Quiz Results
1. After taking a quiz, view your results page
2. Click the **"Share"** button (top-right, purple)
3. Same sharing options as flashcards:
   - Make public or share with specific users

### 3.4 How to Use Public Links
**Creating:**
1. Click **"Share"** button
2. Toggle **"Make Public"** to ON
3. Copy the generated link
4. Share it anywhere (email, messaging, etc.)

**Using (as recipient):**
- Paste the link in your browser
- View the content without login
- Click **"Copy to Library"** to duplicate it to your own account

### 3.5 Sharing with Specific Users
**Sending:**
1. Click **"Share"** button
2. Go to **"Share with Users"** tab
3. Search for users by email
4. Click **"Add User"** for each person
5. Click **"Share"** to send them access
6. ✅ They'll see it in their **"Shared with Me"** page

**Receiving:**
1. Go to **"Shared with Me"** in the sidebar
2. You'll see all content shared with you
3. Click **"View"** to preview
4. Click **"Copy to Library"** to own a copy

## 📖 Feature 4: View Shared Content

### 4.1 Access "Shared with Me"
1. Click **"Shared with Me"** in the left sidebar
2. Switch between tabs:
   - **Flashcard Sets**: All flashcards shared with you
   - **Quiz Sets**: All quizzes shared with you
3. For each item, you can:
   - Click **"View"** to preview the content
   - Click **"Copy to Library"** to duplicate it (you get full ownership)

### 4.2 View from Study Groups
1. Go to your group
2. In **Shared Materials** section, view all materials added by group members
3. Click on a material to open it in detail view
4. Study the content (you have read-only access)

## 💡 Tips & Best Practices

1. **Finding Material IDs**
   - Go to your Lectures page
   - Open a lecture
   - View the flashcards or quizzes
   - Copy the ID from the detail view

2. **Public vs Targeted Sharing**
   - Use **Public** for open collaboration and sharing with many people
   - Use **Targeted** when you want to share only with specific users

3. **Study Group Organization**
   - Create groups by course or topic
   - Have group members add their best materials
   - Use group materials for collaborative learning

4. **Backing Up Content**
   - Share important content publicly or with specific users
   - Use "Copy to Library" to own copies of shared content
   - This ensures you always have your study materials

## ❓ Common Tasks

### "I want to study flashcards created by my group members"
1. Go to Study Groups
2. Open your group
3. In Shared Materials, view the flashcard sets
4. Click on one to study it

### "I want to let my friend use my flashcards without giving them access to my account"
1. Go to your lecture
2. Click Share
3. Copy the public link
4. Send it to your friend
5. They can view it without logging in and copy it to their account

### "My study group needs a centralized place for everyone's materials"
1. Create a Study Group
2. Invite all group members
3. Have each member add their best materials to the group
4. Everyone can now access and study together

### "I received some shared flashcards and want to own them"
1. Go to Shared with Me
2. Find the flashcard set
3. Click "Copy to Library"
4. Now you have your own copy in your Lectures!

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

**Problem**: I don't see the Share button
- Solution: Make sure you're on the correct page (Lecture, Flashcard Review, or Quiz Results)

**Problem**: Invited user doesn't see the group
- Solution: Check that you entered their email correctly. They should see it in their Study Groups list.

**Problem**: Material IDs aren't working
- Solution: Copy the exact ID from your flashcard/quiz detail page. IDs should be full alphanumeric strings.

**Problem**: Can't remove a member
- Solution: Make sure you're the group owner. Only owners can remove members.
